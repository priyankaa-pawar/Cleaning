What cleaning services can I book?

We offer full home deep cleaning, kitchen deep cleaning, office deep cleaning, window cleaning, sofa cleaning, carpet cleaning and floor cleaning.

How do I get a price for my cleaning?

Send us the service you need, your location and a few details about the space on WhatsApp. Our team will get back to you with a tailored quote.

Can I book more than one service?

Yes. Let us know which areas or surfaces you would like cleaned and we can discuss the right combination for your space.

How do I choose the right service?

Tell us about your space and what you would like cleaned. We can help you decide whether you need a full home clean or a specialist service.

How long does a full home deep clean take?

The time depends on the size and condition of your home. Share a few details when requesting your quote and we can advise on timing.

What information should I share for a quote?

Share your location, preferred service, approximate size of the space and any particular areas you would like us to focus on.

Which areas do you cover?
